Welcome to Hope Fountain Combined School

Welcome to Hope Fountain Combined School. We are a school based in Hillbrow, Johannesburg. Our aim is to provide learners with a positive environment where they can learn and grow.

The school provides learners with academic opportunities as well as sports and other activities.
