About Our School

Hope Fountain Combined School is a school based in Hillbrow, Johannesburg.

The school provides education and learning opportunities for learners in the community. It aims to create a safe and positive environment where learners can develop.

The school encourages learners to work hard, respect others and take part in school activities.

Our History

Hope Fountain Combined School serves learners in the Hillbrow community. The school focuses on education and helping learners prepare for their future.

The school works to support learners in both their academic work and activities outside the classroom.

Our Mission

To support learners through education and help them develop their skills, knowledge and confidence.

Our Vision

To create a positive learning environment where learners can work towards a better future.

Our Community

We aim to build a strong relationship between learners, parents, teachers and the community.

Our Values

Hope Fountain Combined School encourages learners to follow important values such as:

  • Respect
  • Teamwork
  • Discipline
  • Responsibility
  • Hard work
