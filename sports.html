Sports and Activities

Sports are an important part of school life. They can help learners stay active, work as a team and develop confidence.

Taking part in sports can also help learners develop discipline, communication and teamwork skills.

Our Sports

Soccer

Soccer encourages teamwork, fitness and discipline. Learners can develop their skills while working together.

Athletics

Athletics allows learners to improve their fitness and take part in different competitions.

Netball

Netball encourages teamwork, communication and cooperation between learners.

Benefits of Sports

  • Encourages teamwork
  • Improves fitness
  • Builds confidence
  • Develops discipline
  • Encourages communication
